Every figure in this program is illustrative and dated. This page is how you check it.

## Start with the primary source

- **VA home loans hub** — [va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/)
- **Request a Certificate of Eligibility** — [va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/how-to-request-coe](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/how-to-request-coe/)
- **Current funding fee rates** — [va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/)
- **VA loan limits and entitlement** — [va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/)
- **VA Lenders Handbook (M26-7)** — the underwriting rules themselves, including residual income tables: [benefits.va.gov/warms/pam26_7.asp](https://www.benefits.va.gov/warms/pam26_7.asp)
- **VA Regional Loan Center: 1-877-827-3702** — free and authoritative. Call them with entitlement questions before you call anyone else.

## Forms you will actually use

| Form | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **VA Form 26-1880** | Request for a Certificate of Eligibility / restoration of entitlement |
| **VA Form 26-1817** | Request for COE for a surviving spouse |
| **DD-214** | Discharge documentation |
| **NGB-22** | Guard service documentation |
| **Statement of Service** | For active-duty members, signed by the command |
| **Fannie Mae Form 1007 / 1025** | Single-family and small residential income property rent schedules, used to document market rent |

- **Your state or local landlord association** — leases, screening tools, legal updates
- **Your local building department** — permit history and legal unit counts
- **Your county assessor** — reassessment policy and exemption rules

## Getting help if things go wrong

If you are struggling with mortgage payments, **call the VA at 1-877-827-3702 before you miss a payment.** VA loan technicians can intervene with your servicer on your behalf — for VA-guaranteed loans, and in some cases for non-VA loans held by veterans. This is a real service that has kept many veterans out of foreclosure, and it is free.

The VA never charges for COE processing, and no legitimate party requires an up-front fee to "unlock" your benefit.

:::warning Scams targeting veterans
Be skeptical of: unsolicited refinance offers that mimic official VA or government mailings; "veteran benefit" seminars that end in a high-priced coaching upsell; anyone who asks for money to obtain your COE; and lenders who pressure you to waive inspections or sign blank documents. Report suspected fraud to the VA and the CFPB.
:::
