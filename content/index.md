## What this program is

A structured, self-paced course. Each module has learning objectives, the actual rules (with the numbers), worked examples, calculators, and an action checklist you can complete on this site — your progress is saved in your browser.

It is written for the veteran, service member, or surviving spouse who wants more than a house: who wants the house to be the first unit of a portfolio, and who has more time and discipline than cash.

## The core strategy, in one page

The VA benefit is not a one-time coupon. It is a **renewable guaranty** that follows you. The empire is built by repeating a four-step cycle:

1. **Buy** a 1–4 unit property with a VA loan, $0 down, and move in. You must genuinely intend to occupy it as your primary residence.
2. **Live** in one unit while the other units (or roommates) pay down your mortgage. Your housing cost collapses — often to zero or less.
3. **Move** after your occupancy obligation is genuinely satisfied — a PCS, a job change, a growing family, or simply after a year of good-faith residence. Keep the property. Rent your unit out too.
4. **Repeat** using remaining entitlement, restored entitlement, or non-VA financing — with a new property, more doors, and the rental income from the last one now counting toward qualification.

Each cycle adds doors, adds cash flow, adds equity, and makes the next cycle easier to qualify for. Four cycles in eight years, at four units each, is sixteen doors — bought with almost no down payment capital.

:::mission The discipline that makes it work
Nothing here works if you buy a bad deal. The VA benefit removes the down payment barrier; it does not remove the math. Modules 6 and 7 exist because a zero-down purchase of an overpriced property is a faster way to lose than a rented apartment.
:::

## Who is eligible

Eligibility generally requires qualifying service — active duty, National Guard, Reserves, or veteran status with sufficient service time — or being an eligible surviving spouse. Service requirements vary by era and component, and the VA is the only authority on your specific case. Module 1 covers how to pull your Certificate of Eligibility.
