## VA loan terms

**Basic entitlement** — The legacy $36,000 portion of the VA guaranty available to every eligible veteran.

**Bonus (Tier 2) entitlement** — Additional entitlement above basic, bringing total guaranty capacity to 25% of the applicable county conforming loan limit.

**Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** — The VA document establishing that you are eligible and showing how much entitlement is available or already charged.

**Entitlement** — The dollar amount of the VA's guaranty available to back your loans. Charged when you use it; restorable under specific conditions.

**Entitlement charged** — The portion of your entitlement committed to an outstanding VA loan. Read it off your COE; it is not always 25% of the original loan amount.

**Funding fee** — A one-time percentage-of-loan fee that funds the VA loan program. Financeable into the loan. Waived for veterans receiving compensation for a service-connected disability and certain others.

**Guaranty** — The VA's promise to indemnify the lender for a portion of loss on default, typically 25% of the loan. This is what replaces the down payment.

**IRRRL** — Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan, the VA streamline refinance. Low documentation, 0.50% funding fee, and it only requires that you *previously* occupied the property — which makes it usable on a former residence now rented.

**Minimum Property Requirements (MPRs)** — VA standards that a property be safe, structurally sound, and sanitary. Deficiencies must be corrected before closing.

**NADL** — Native American Direct Loan, a VA program where the VA lends directly for homes on federal trust land.

**Notice of Value (NOV)** — The VA's determination of reasonable value from the appraisal, plus any required repair conditions. The loan is sized against this, not the contract price.

**One-time restoration** — A single opportunity to have entitlement restored after paying a VA loan in full **without** disposing of the property.

**Residual income** — Money left over each month after PITI, debts, taxes, and estimated utilities. The VA requires a minimum amount by family size, region, and loan size — a hallmark of VA underwriting.

**Restoration of entitlement** — Recovering used entitlement, typically after selling and paying off, using the one-time restoration, or through substitution of entitlement.

**Second-tier entitlement** — Using remaining entitlement to obtain a second VA loan while keeping the first property. See Module 2.

**Substitution of entitlement** — An eligible veteran assumes your VA loan and substitutes their entitlement for yours, releasing yours.

**Tidewater Initiative** — A process giving the lender and agent a short window to submit additional comparable sales when a VA appraisal is trending below the contract price.

**VA Amendatory Escape Clause** — A mandatory contract provision allowing a VA buyer to withdraw and recover earnest money if the property appraises below the contract price. It cannot be waived.

**WebLGY** — The VA's system through which approved lenders pull a COE, often in minutes.

## Investing and lending terms

**Break-even occupancy** — The occupancy rate at which income exactly covers operating expenses plus debt service.

**Cap rate** — NOI divided by price. An unlevered yield measure used to compare properties.

**Cash-on-cash return** — Annual pre-tax cash flow divided by cash invested.

**CapEx (capital expenditures)** — Large, periodic replacements: roof, HVAC, water heater, parking, windows. Distinct from routine maintenance and budgeted separately.

**Concessions** — Seller-paid items beyond ordinary closing costs — prepaid taxes and insurance, paying off buyer debts, appliances, the funding fee. Capped at 4% of established reasonable value on VA loans.

**Conforming loan limit** — The FHFA limit published annually. Sets VA guaranty capacity when you have partial entitlement.

**DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)** — NOI divided by annual debt service. Also the name of a loan product that qualifies on this ratio instead of your personal income.

**DTI (debt-to-income)** — Total monthly debt payments including the proposed mortgage, divided by gross monthly qualifying income. VA guideline benchmark is 41%.

**Due-on-sale clause** — A mortgage provision letting the lender call the loan if the property is transferred — relevant when considering moving a property into an LLC.

**EGI (effective gross income)** — Gross scheduled rent less vacancy and credit loss.

**EEM (Energy Efficient Mortgage)** — Additional loan funds (typically up to $6,000 on a VA loan) for qualifying energy improvements.

**Estoppel certificate** — A tenant-signed statement confirming rent, deposit, lease term, and any side agreements. Essential when buying an occupied building.

**Fair housing** — Federal, state, and local laws prohibiting discrimination in housing. Protected classes vary by jurisdiction; many add source of income and military status.

**House hack** — Buying a property, occupying part of it, and renting the rest.

**MPR** — See Minimum Property Requirements.

**NOI (Net Operating Income)** — EGI minus operating expenses, before debt service. Describes the property independent of financing.

**PITI** — Principal, interest, taxes, and insurance: the full monthly housing payment.

**Proforma** — Projected financials. Treat as marketing until verified against leases and bank deposits.

**Reconsideration of Value (ROV)** — A request, supported by evidence, to revisit an appraised value.

**RUBS (Ratio Utility Billing System)** — Allocating owner-paid utilities to tenants by formula where legally permitted.

**Rent roll** — A schedule of units, tenants, rents, lease terms, and deposits.

**Schedule E** — The IRS form reporting rental income and expenses. Two years of it is what lets future lenders count rental income.

**Turnover** — The cost and vacancy between one tenant leaving and the next moving in.

**1031 exchange** — A tax-deferred exchange of investment property for like-kind property under strict identification and closing timelines.
