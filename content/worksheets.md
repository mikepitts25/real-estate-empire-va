## 4. Offer strategy

- [ ] Comparable sales pulled to support the price
- [ ] Seller-paid closing costs quantified with the lender
- [ ] Lender credit option priced
- [ ] Underwritten pre-approval letter attached
- [ ] Lender's direct phone number included for the listing agent
- [ ] Earnest money amount and source confirmed
- [ ] Inspection contingency timeline set
- [ ] Path to a legally vacant unit written into the contract
- [ ] Closing date realistic for a VA appraisal in this market

## 5. Due diligence checklist

- [ ] General inspection ordered (access arranged to every unit)
- [ ] Sewer scope ordered
- [ ] Roof inspected by a roofer
- [ ] Electrical panel evaluated
- [ ] WDI / termite inspection (where applicable)
- [ ] Radon and lead paint (pre-1978)
- [ ] Current leases collected for every unit
- [ ] Estoppel certificates signed by every tenant
- [ ] Security deposit accounting obtained
- [ ] 12 months of rent ledgers / bank deposits reviewed
- [ ] Utility bills for 24 months reviewed; meter configuration confirmed
- [ ] Permit history checked at the building department
- [ ] Actual insurance quote obtained (not an estimate)
- [ ] County assessor contacted about post-sale reassessment
- [ ] Rental licensing requirements confirmed
- [ ] MPR repair items identified and assigned to seller or buyer


- [ ] Criteria written down and applied identically to every applicant
- [ ] Fair housing course completed
- [ ] Screening service selected, with applicant consent forms
- [ ] Prior landlord (not just current) contacted
- [ ] Income verified with documents, not screenshots
- [ ] Every denial documented against the written criteria

## 7. Move-out / conversion checklist

- [ ] Reason for move documented and filed (orders, offer letter, etc.)
- [ ] Unit marketed 45-60 days before vacating
- [ ] Lease signed with rent starting within a week of vacancy
- [ ] Homeowner policy converted to landlord / dwelling fire
- [ ] Umbrella liability policy reviewed
- [ ] Owner-occupancy tax exemption removed
- [ ] Rental license obtained / property registered
- [ ] Loan servicer mailing address updated
- [ ] Management decision made: self-manage or hire
- [ ] Compliance file complete for this property
